# OEM Manual Extract — Slurry Pump Mechanical Seal and Bearings

## Seal flushing
- Clean flush water at 1 bar above stuffing box pressure, 3–5 l/min.
- Loss of flush water causes dry running and seal face damage within hours on abrasive slurry.

## Seal replacement
1. Isolate suction and discharge valves; drain the casing.
2. Remove coupling spacer and bearing frame.
3. Replace the cartridge seal; never reuse seal faces.
4. Verify flush line is clear before restart; never run dry.

## Bearing housing
- Oil level 60–80% of sight glass, ISO VG 68.
- Hardened lip seals cause oil leakage from the housing; replace with every bearing change.

## Cavitation
- Gravel-like noise, fluctuating discharge pressure, pitted impeller.
- Causes: low suction tank level, choked suction strainer, partly closed suction valve, air ingress.

## Impeller choking
- Fibrous digestate wraps impeller vanes, causing imbalance and low steady discharge pressure.
- Back-flush, open casing, clean vanes, clean suction strainer.
