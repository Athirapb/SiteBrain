# SOP-SCR-02 — H2S Scrubber De-choking, Media Re-seating and Flushing

Applies to: H2S Scrubber Vessel (SCRUBBER-H2S-01, dry iron-oxide / impregnated activated-carbon media) and the
downstream Gas Filtration Skid.

## 1. Triggers
- Differential pressure (dP) spike above 0.10 bar, or a sudden dP drop below 0.02 bar (bed channeling). Normal 0.04–0.06 bar.
- Downstream sulfur breakthrough above 10 ppm H2S at the scrubber outlet analyzer (confirm with a Dräger tube).
- Media granules or black powder found in the filter skid drain or compressor suction strainer.
- Hot spot on the vessel shell (> 60 °C) or outlet O2 above 1% — see section 7 before doing anything else.

## 2. Safety prerequisites
- Interlock SCR-01: downstream filter bypass valve must be locked CLOSED before opening the vessel.
- If solid particle carryover is suspected, interlock CMP-01 trips the LP compressor load line. Do not reset it until the suction vessel strainer is inspected and clean.
- Advisory SCR-02: wear an H2S personal monitor whenever outlet H2S is high or a sample point / drain is opened.
- Depressurize, isolate with spectacle blinds, purge with nitrogen to <10% LEL and O2 > 19.5% before entry.
- Confined space permit, H2S personal monitor, standby attendant, rescue plan.
- **Pyrophoric media:** spent iron-oxide media contains iron sulfide that heats up and can smoulder when it meets air.
  Keep exposed media wet (water spray), remove it in small batches into closed, wetted drums, and never leave a dry pile.
  Have a charged water hose at the manway during all media work.

## 3. Primary checkpoint — top distributor mesh
1. Open the top manway and inspect the distributor mesh alignment (from the manway; entry only under permit).
2. Normal baseline: 100% perimeter contact with the vessel wall; all mesh fasteners torqued.
3. Fault indicators: perimeter seal gap, displaced or torn mesh, media heaped on one side (channeling),
   yellow sulfur crust or lumps under the mesh (caking).

## 4. Media re-seating
1. Vacuum out the top 150 mm of media (keep it wet); inspect for caking or fused sulfur lumps.
2. Level the bed; replace caked media with fresh, screened media of the same grade.
3. Re-align the top distributor mesh, fit a new perimeter seal strip (EPDM/Viton), torque 316 SS fasteners to 25 N·m in a star pattern.
4. Acceptance: photograph the full perimeter, record torque values, second-person sign-off on the work order.

## 5. De-choking (dP climbing steadily)
1. Reduce gas flow to 50% and confirm the dP trend over 30 minutes.
2. Back-flush the media with dry nitrogen at 0.5 bar for 20 minutes through the bottom nozzle.
3. Check upstream moisture knock-out drain — moisture is the main cause of media caking.
4. Check air-dosing: outlet O2 above 0.5% deposits elemental sulfur in the bed and speeds up caking.
5. If dP does not recover below 0.08 bar, replace the caked layer (section 4) or the full charge.

## 6. Media change-out (exhaustion / breakthrough)
1. Switch to the standby vessel or reduce flow; confirm breakthrough with a tube sample.
2. Plan change-out on sulfur loading: daily log of gas flow × inlet H2S; order media at 70% of rated capacity.
3. Remove spent media wet (section 2); dispose as per the hazardous waste authorisation.
4. Screen or blow down new media to remove fines before closing up; run the filter skid on its standby element for the first week.
5. Re-check bed level one month after a fresh charge (settling) and top up.

## 7. Hot spot / excess oxygen
1. Stop air dosing, keep gas flowing (biogas has no O2 to feed the reaction), monitor shell temperature every 15 minutes.
2. Never open a hot vessel or admit air. Inform the shift engineer.
3. Reset dosing to 0.3–0.5% outlet O2 only after the shell is back to gas temperature.

## 8. Flushing and return to service
1. Flush the downstream filter skid housing and clean the compressor suction strainers.
2. Pressure test the vessel at 1.1× operating pressure, soap-test manway joints.
3. Pressurise slowly through the restriction orifice — fast surges lift and disturb the bed.
4. Verify dP 0.04–0.06 bar and outlet H2S below 10 ppm for one hour before releasing SCR-01 and CMP-01.

## 9. Records
Work order with photos, torque sheet, media lot number and quantity, spent media disposal note, dP / H2S readings at release.
