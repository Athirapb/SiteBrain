# SOP-SLS-01 — Separation Performance and Polymer Dosing

Area: Solid-Liquid Separation (SLS). Equipment: Decanter 1–3, WAM 1–5, polymer make-up unit, decanter feed pumps.

## 1. Purpose
Keep cake dryness, centrate / filtrate quality and machine reliability within target by controlling feed,
polymer and machine settings in a consistent, recorded way.

## 2. Targets
| Parameter | Target |
|---|---|
| Feed TS | 3–8 %, stable within ± 1 % per shift |
| Decanter cake | 25–35 % DS |
| Decanter centrate | < 0.5–1 % TSS, clear to light brown |
| Solids capture | ≥ 90 % (decanter), 85–92 % (screw press) |
| Polymer dose | 6–10 kg active per tonne dry solids (decanter) |
| Polymer solution | 0.1–0.3 % active, used within 24 h |
| WAM cake | 25–35 % DM |

## 3. Polymer make-up
1. Use the posted recipe: kg powder (or litres emulsion) per m³ of water for the target concentration.
2. Powder must be dry and free-flowing. Discard lumped / caked powder. Store bags sealed, on pallets, in a dry room.
3. Feed powder slowly into the wetting head with full water flow — never dump it into the tank.
4. Mix gently for the maturing time (typically 30–60 min). Good solution is smooth and stringy with no fish-eyes.
5. Log every batch: time, quantity, water volume, operator. Discard any batch older than 24 h.
6. Clean the wetting head and mixer monthly.

## 4. Setting the dose
1. Dose (kg/h) = feed flow (m³/h) × feed TS (%) × 10 × target dose (kg/t DS) / 1000.
2. Calibrate the dosing pump monthly with a drawdown (calibration column) test.
3. Jar test weekly and whenever the feedstock mix changes: add solution to feed samples at 3–4 doses; pick the
   lowest dose that gives large, firm flocs and clear liquid.
4. Signs of underdosing: dirty centrate, wet cake, low torque. Overdosing: slimy cake, foam in CDT, high cost.

## 5. Decanter settings
- Keep a **setpoint log** for each decanter: feed flow, polymer dose, bowl speed, differential speed, pond depth.
- Changes need supervisor approval and must be logged with the reason and result.
- Adjust one setting at a time in small steps; wait 15 min before judging.
- Lower differential = drier cake but higher torque. Do not chase dryness by dropping differential at high feed TS.
- Retune when feed TS changes by more than 1 %.

## 6. Shift checks
- Feed flow and TS (grab sample once per shift), cake appearance, centrate in a clear bottle.
- Torque, differential, vibration and bearing temperatures on each decanter.
- Lubricator level, gearbox oil level and leaks.
- WAM spray pattern, wash pressure, inlet level, cake and filtrate.
- Polymer batch age and dosing pump flow.

## 7. Stops and restarts
- Every planned decanter stop uses the PLC flush sequence.
- After any trip / power failure: flush at low speed before restart; restore feed at 50 % and ramp up.
- When a separator is out of service, reduce total SLS feed — never push its share onto the others beyond rating.

## 8. Coordination with digesters and CDT/PST
- Feed tank (PST) agitator must run whenever the SLS is fed. Minimum feed tank level: 30 %.
- Feedstock or digestate TS changes must be written on the shift handover sheet.

## 9. Safety
- LOTO per SOP-ROT-01 before any intervention. Decanters coast 10–20 min after stop.
- Polymer is slippery when wet: clean spills with absorbent, not water.
- Wear gloves and goggles when handling polymer powder or solution.
