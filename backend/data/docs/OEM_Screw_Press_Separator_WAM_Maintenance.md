# OEM Manual Extract — Screw-Press / Screen Separator (WAM 1–5)

Applies to: SLS WAM 1–5 (screw-press screen separators of the WAM / Sepcom type). Compiled from screw-press
separator OEM literature, biogas operator training material and plant experience.

## How it works
- Digestate enters the inlet box and is pushed by a slow screw along a cylindrical screen (mesh / wedge-wire,
  typically 0.25–1 mm openings).
- Liquid drains through the screen by gravity and pressure; the liquid fraction (filtrate) goes to the CDT.
- At the outlet, a counter-pressure flap (weights or pneumatic cylinder) holds back the solids plug so it is
  pressed to about **25–35 % dry matter**.
- A brush or the screw flight edge scrapes the screen; a wash spray bar keeps the mesh open.

## Normal operation
| Item | Normal |
|---|---|
| Feed flow | At or below nameplate rating; reduce for thick feed |
| Wash water pressure | ≥ 2 bar at the spray bar during wash cycles |
| Cake | Firm, crumbly, 25–35 % DM, steady discharge |
| Filtrate | Brown liquid with fines, no visible fibres |
| Motor current | ≤ nameplate FLA (≈ 18 A typical here), steady |
| Vibration | < 4.5 mm/s |

## Wash system
- Wash runs on a timer (e.g. 10–30 s every 5–15 min, per commissioning setting) through a solenoid valve.
- Use clean service water. Recycled filtrate chokes nozzles unless finely filtered.
- Clean the line strainer weekly; fit a pressure gauge after the strainer.
- Check the spray pattern each shift: it must cover the full screen width.

## Counter-pressure setting
- More counter-pressure → drier cake, but higher motor load and risk of plugging the outlet.
- Less counter-pressure → wetter cake, more throughput.
- Mark the commissioning setting on the machine. Change only in small steps and record in the setpoint log.

## Preventive maintenance
| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every shift | Spray pattern, wash pressure, inlet level, cake dryness, filtrate, motor current |
| Weekly | Clean wash strainer and nozzles; inspect screen via hatch (LOTO); clean inlet box |
| Monthly | Function-test wash solenoid; check counter-pressure regulator and gauge; gearmotor oil level |
| Every 500 h | Measure screw / brush to screen gap; replace brush strips if worn |
| 2,000–3,000 h | Replace screen basket (or per condition) |
| Yearly | Gearmotor oil change, bearing check, motor insulation test |

## Troubleshooting quick table
| Symptom | Likely cause | First action |
|---|---|---|
| Low throughput, inlet backing up | Screen blinded (low wash, choked nozzles, worn brush) | Check spray and wash pressure; inspect screen under LOTO |
| Wet cake | Counter-pressure too low, overfeeding, torn screen | Check flap setting and feed rate |
| Outlet plugged, high current | Counter-pressure too high, fibres / plastics | Reduce pressure; clear under LOTO |
| Fibres in filtrate | Torn screen | Stop and replace screen |
| Overload trips | Overfeeding, wrapped debris, gearmotor fault | Check feed, screw and gearbox |
| Some nozzles dead | Nozzles choked by solids or scale | Remove and clean spray bar |

## Safety
- Stop the separator AND the feed pump and apply LOTO (SOP-ROT-01) before opening the inspection hatch or
  clearing a blockage. The screw can restart automatically on level control.
- Never put hands into the inlet box or outlet while the screw can turn.
- Do not use a high-pressure lance on fine screens — it tears the mesh.
- Digestate areas can contain H2S; wear a personal gas monitor.
