# SOP-ROT-01 — Lock-Out / Tag-Out for Rotating Equipment

Applies to: agitators, pumps, blowers, compressors, decanters, WAM screens, shredders, ID fans.

## When LOTO is mandatory
- Any inspection of Critical vibration or abnormal sound.
- Belt, coupling, seal, gland or impeller work.
- Opening any guard or casing.

## Procedure
1. Inform the control room operator and record the work in the LOTO register.
2. Stop the equipment from the local push button, then open and lock the MCC isolator.
3. Apply personal lock and danger tag; keep the key with you.
4. Try-start from the local station to prove zero energy.
5. For pumps: close and lock suction and discharge valves; drain the casing.
6. For agitators: confirm the shaft has stopped; lower tank level if impeller access is needed.
7. On completion: remove tools, refit guards, remove locks in reverse order, inform control room, restart and trend vibration.

## Vibration limits (ISO 10816 class II guidance)
- Good: below 2.8 mm/s RMS
- Acceptable: 2.8–4.5 mm/s
- Alert (Abnormal): 4.5–7.1 mm/s — trend hourly
- Danger (Critical): above 7.1 mm/s — stop and apply LOTO
